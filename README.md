Hi, I'm Shivank: a senior full-stack engineer by trade, and a compulsive builder by habit.

### How I think

**Measure first, then optimise.** Guessing where the slow part is is a hobby, not a method. Every speed-up I've shipped started with a number I wanted to move.

**Loops are suspicious until proven innocent.** At Caissa Labs, one batched call replaced a hundred sequential ones. Analysis went from 3 seconds per game to 50 games in 4 seconds.

**The last few percent is a business decision.** I once settled for 87% clustering accuracy instead of 95%, because those extra points cost more compute than they were worth. Not every decimal deserves to be chased.

**Ship the small version.** A v1 in people's hands beats a perfect v2 in my head. I have about forty repos to prove I take this one seriously.
